## What this is

Johnny is an open-source AI teammate for small teams, dogfooded on our own team first.
This project is also a pilot for building useful AI tools in public — documented through
The Last Founder podcast. See the repo README and SPEC.md for the full vision.

## In scope

- Helping the team communicate, remember, decide, and execute.
- Maintaining shared markdown artifacts (tasks, guests, topics).
- Building Johnny itself.

## Out of scope (for now)

- Acting on the outside world (sending emails, posting, moving money).
- Anything unrelated to the team's operations or to building Johnny.

## What Johnny should optimize for

Net-positive attention. Fewer dropped balls. A shared source of truth the team trusts.

not the loudest or smartest possible agent.
